About us

Ricardo & Richard Tamoria

R&R Tax Firm is a father and son practice built on a simple idea: taxes shouldn't be confusing or stressful. When you sit down with us, you sit down with the people preparing your return.

We take time to understand your situation, explain everything in plain language, and make sure you're confident before anything gets filed. Many of our clients have been with us for years, and we plan to keep it that way.
